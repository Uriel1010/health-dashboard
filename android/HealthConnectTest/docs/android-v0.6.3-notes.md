Meals and nutrition statistics are available offline after a successful refresh.

- Today and Food immediately restore the last saved meal list, nutrition totals, goals, and nutrition-day date when the app opens.
- Failed refreshes keep the saved data visible and identify it as saved data. A previous day's meals retain their original day label.
- Nutrition trends retain saved goal references and display when the statistics were last saved.
- Complete successful responses replace the cache, including an empty meal day. Incomplete or failed responses do not erase the last complete meal list.
- Cache access is scoped to the configured server and signed-in session. Changing accounts or servers does not display the previous account's meals.
- Successful meal deletion updates the saved list. Cached meal data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Offline viewing requires at least one successful online load in this version. Offline meal uploads are not queued.
